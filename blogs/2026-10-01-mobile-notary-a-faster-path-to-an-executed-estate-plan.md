A finalized estate plan isn’t a done deal. Until it’s signed, witnessed, and notarized, it’s still a draft, and getting there is often where things stall. Coordinating notary appointments around bank hours, or a trip to a retail store, is a friction point that can sit between “finalized” and “funded” for weeks.
